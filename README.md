## 📊 How the AI Learns

The magic behind this project is called **Deep Reinforcement Learning**. Here's how it works in simple terms:

1. **Exploration** – The AI starts by taking random actions (steering, accelerating, braking).
2. **Rewards** – The software gives the AI positive points for good driving (staying in lane, obeying lights) and negative points for mistakes (hitting obstacles).
3. **Learning** – Over thousands of attempts, the AI learns which actions lead to the most rewards.
4. **Improvement** – The AI becomes a skilled driver without anyone writing explicit instructions.

The project uses two advanced AI algorithms:
- **Dueling DQN** – Helps the AI evaluate which actions are best in different situations.
- **PPO** – Makes the learning process more stable and efficient.

Additionally, a **Variational Autoencoder (VAE)** compresses visual information, allowing the AI to process road scenes faster and more accurately.
